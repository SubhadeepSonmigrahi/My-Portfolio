Exploratory Data Analysis & Statistical Modeling using R

Understanding the relationship between social media usage, mental health, and academic performance.

Analysed data from 705 students across 110 countries to investigate the impact of social media usage on mental health and academic performance.

Identified a strong negative correlation (r = -0.801) between daily social media usage and mental health scores. Segmented high-risk students using multivariate analysis and validated findings through Chi-Square testing.
